Bank Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

Overview

Customer churn is a critical challenge for banks because retaining existing customers is significantly more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.

This project analyzes 10,000 customer accounts from a European bank to identify patterns associated with customer churn and explore whether churn can be predicted using available customer attributes.

The dashboard was built using Power BI to provide interactive insights into customer demographics, geographic trends, credit score behavior, and customer segmentation.

The goal is to help stakeholders understand why customers leave and how the bank can improve retention strategies.

Dataset Description

The dataset contains 10,000 bank customers with information related to demographics, financial behavior, and account activity.

Business Questions Answered
1️. What attributes are more common among churners?

Several attributes appear more frequently among customers who churn:

Lower customer engagement levels
Certain geographic regions
Variations in credit score ranges
Differences in account balances

Understanding these attributes helps the bank identify early churn indicators.

2️. Can churn be predicted using the variables in the dataset?

Yes. Several variables in the dataset can be used for predictive churn modeling, including:

Credit Score
Customer Age
Account Balance
Active Member Status
Number of Products

These variables provide a strong foundation for machine learning churn prediction models in future work.

3️. What do the overall demographics of the bank’s customers look like?

The customer base is distributed across:

France
Germany
Spain

Customers vary across multiple demographic attributes including gender, age groups, and engagement levels, offering a comprehensive view of the bank's customer structure.

4️. Is there a difference in behavior between German, French, and Spanish customers?

Yes. The analysis reveals:

Differences in customer distribution by country
Variation in average account balances
Differences in churn proportions across regions

These insights help identify regional customer behavior patterns.

5️. What customer segments exist within the bank?

Customers were grouped into the following segments:

Regular Customers
High Value Customers
At Risk Customers
Low Engagement Customers

Segment analysis helps identify which groups require targeted retention strategies.

Insights
1 The overall customer churn rate is approximately 20%.
2 Customer behavior differs significantly across geographic regions.
3 Low engagement customers show higher churn probability.
4 Credit score and engagement levels play a major role in customer retention.

These insights help the bank design data-driven customer retention strategies.
